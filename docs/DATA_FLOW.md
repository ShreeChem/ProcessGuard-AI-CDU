# Data Flow

## Inputs

- Synthetic crude feed, temperatures, pressures, flows and equipment performance values.
- Scenario selection and severity.
- Demonstration setpoints and speed.

## Processing

- Scenario-specific process-response targets.
- Noise and gradual response dynamics.
- Residual calculation (for example, actual firing versus expected firing).
- Deviation scoring and conventional limit comparison.
- Evidence ranking, cause-effect mapping and simplified forecasts.

## Outputs

- Live PFD-style values.
- Trends and typical operating ranges.
- ProcessGuard deviation state and DCS-alarm comparison.
- Evidence matrix and diagnostic ranking.
- Recommended operator checks.
- Product redistribution and energy impact.
- What-if and replay views.

All displayed operating values are synthetic demonstrations unless a future validated plant connector is added.
